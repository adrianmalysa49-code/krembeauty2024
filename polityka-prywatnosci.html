2. What data we process and why

  • Phone contact and booking appointments. If you call us, we process the name and phone number you provide — in order to arrange and deliver the service and to call you back. Online booking is handled by the external service Booksy, which is a separate controller of your data.
  • Chat assistant on the website. We process the content of the messages you type in the chat window — in order to answer questions about services, prices, or opening hours.   • Google Fonts. The site loads fonts from Google's servers (Google Fonts), which may involve transmitting your IP address to Google.

3. Legal bases for processing

We process data on the basis of the GDPR:

  • Art. 6(1)(b) — taking steps at your request prior to entering into a contract and performing the service (contact, booking);
  • Art. 6(1)(f) — our legitimate interest: handling chat enquiries, keeping the website functional and secure, and defending against potential claims.

4. Recipients of data

To the extent necessary for the website to work, your data may be entrusted to trusted providers:

  • Vercel Inc. — website hosting (USA);
  • Anthropic PBC — the chat assistant on the site (USA);
  • Google — fonts (Google Fonts) and Google Maps;
  • Booksy — online booking system (if you use it);
  • Meta Platforms — Instagram and Facebook: clicking our links takes you to services that process data under their own rules.

We do not sell your personal data.

5. Transfers outside the EEA

Some of the services we use (Vercel, Anthropic, Google) are based in the United States, so data may be transferred outside the European Economic Area. This takes place on the basis of safeguards provided for in the GDPR — Standard Contractual Clauses and/or the Data Privacy Framework.

6. Retention period

  • contact and booking data — for as long as necessary to deliver the service and for the period of limitation of any claims;
  • technical logs — for the period resulting from the hosting provider's settings;
  • chat messages — are not permanently stored by us.

7. Cookies and browser storage

The site does not use cookies for marketing or analytics purposes and does not track your activity. It only uses the browser's local storage (localStorage) to remember your chosen site language — this information stays on your device and is not sent to us. You can delete it at any time by clearing your browser data.

8. Your rights

You have the right to: access your data, rectify it, erase it, restrict processing, object, data portability, and withdraw consent. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the supervisory authority — the President of the Personal Data Protection Office (UODO) (ul. Stawki 2, 00-193 Warsaw, Poland).

9. Voluntary provision of data

Providing your data is voluntary but necessary to book an appointment or to receive an answer to your enquiry.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The current version is always available on this page, together with the date of the last update shown at the top.
